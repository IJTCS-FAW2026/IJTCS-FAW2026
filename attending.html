Travel

The nearby metro station is Renmin University (人民大学), served by Lines 4 and 12. Suggested connections from Beijing’s airports and major railway stations are listed below. Check the operators’ current service information before travelling, especially for late arrivals, and use the venue or hotel map for the final walking route.

Arrival pointSuggested metro route
Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK)Capital Airport Express to Sanyuanqiao (三元桥); transfer to Line 12 toward Sijiqing Qiao (四季青桥) and alight at Renmin University.
Beijing Daxing International Airport (PKX)Daxing Airport Express to Caoqiao (草桥); transfer to Line 19 toward Mudanyuan (牡丹园) to Ping’anli (平安里), then Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.
Beijing South Railway Station (北京南站)Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) directly to Renmin University.
Beijing West Railway Station (北京西站)Line 9 toward National Library (国家图书馆); transfer there to Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.
Beijing Fengtai Railway Station (北京丰台站)Line 16 toward Bei’anhe (北安河) to National Library (国家图书馆); transfer to Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.
Beijing Railway Station (北京站)Line 2 via Chongwenmen (崇文门) to Xuanwumen (宣武门); transfer to Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.
Beijing North Railway Station (北京北站)Follow signs to Xizhimen (西直门) metro station; take Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.
Qinghe Railway Station (清河站)Line 13 toward Xizhimen (西直门); transfer there to Line 4 toward Anheqiao Bei (安河桥北) to Renmin University.

These are suggested rail connections, not guaranteed fastest routes. Journey times depend on waiting, transfers, and the final walking route. Participants may also take a licensed taxi to the Chinese venue or hotel address shown on this page.
