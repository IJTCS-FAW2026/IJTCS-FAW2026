Call for Papers

Authors are invited to submit original research papers on the theory of computer science. Submissions should present substantial, original, and unpublished research, and should not be under simultaneous review by another conference or journal.

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to, the following areas.

Algorithms and data structures Approximation and online algorithms Complexity theory Graph algorithms and graph theory Combinatorial optimization Computational geometry Parameterized algorithms Automata theory and formal languages Formal methods and verification Blockchain and distributed ledgers Distributed computing Multi-agent systems Algorithmic game theory Mechanism design Computational social choice Theory of machine learning Theoretical aspects of artificial intelligence Quantum computation Computational biology and bioinformatics Network economics

Tracks

Track A

Frontiers of Algorithmic Wisdom. Algorithms, discrete structures, optimization, graph theory, complexity, and related theoretical foundations.

Track B

Blockchain, distributed computing, and multi-agent systems. Consensus, smart contracts, distributed ledgers, market mechanisms, security, and related models.

Track C

Game theory, algorithmic game theory, machine learning, and electronic commerce. Mechanism design, equilibria, social choice, learning theory, and incentives.
