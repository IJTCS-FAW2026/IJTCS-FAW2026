Overview

International Joint Conference on Theoretical Computer Science – Frontier of Algorithmic Wisdom (IJTCS-FAW 2026) aims to provide a platform for advancing theoretical computer science and promoting the exchange of knowledge and ideas.

The conference calls for papers concerning any branch of theoretical computer science, together with focus tracks in algorithms and complexity, algorithmic game theory, automata theory and formal languages, blockchain, computational biology and bioinformatics, computational geometry, formal methods and verification, graph theory and algorithms, multi-agent systems, network economics, parallel and distributed algorithms, quantum computation, theoretical aspects of artificial intelligence, and theory of machine learning.
