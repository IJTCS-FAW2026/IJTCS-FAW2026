Call for Posters

IJTCS-FAW 2026 invites paper submissions for poster presentation at the conference, to be held on Nov 7–10, 2026, at Renmin University of China in Beijing, China.

Topics and Scope

We welcome papers related to the four IJTCS-FAW 2026 tracks:

  • Frontiers of Algorithmic Wisdom;
  • Blockchain Theory and Technology;
  • Computational Economics and Algorithmic Game Theory; and
  • Foundations of Artificial Intelligence.

Submission and Review

Please submit your paper by email to ijtcsfaw2026pcs@outlook.com, using the subject line “IJTCS-FAW 2026 Poster Submission – [Paper Title].” Include the paper title, author names and affiliations, and a contact email address.

Poster submissions will undergo a brief review by the conference organizers for relevance and suitability for presentation. Authors will be notified of the outcome by email.

Poster submission questions should be directed to the same email address.

This is separate from Full Conference Registration and does not replace the full registration required for an accepted conference paper. See the Registration page for the CCF portal, current opening status, payment instructions, and refund policy.
