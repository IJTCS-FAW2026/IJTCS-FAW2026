Registration

Registration fees for IJTCS-FAW 2026 are listed below. All fees are in Chinese yuan (CNY) and include taxes.

CCF registration portal: IJTCS-FAW 2026 registration. Registration is awaiting activation on the CCF platform; please do not attempt payment until the conference is released there.

The configured registration window is Oct 24–Nov 10, 2026, ending at 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8). Once activated, the CCF portal will accept WeChat Pay and Alipay. Sign in to CCF, open the conference page, and select 报名 (Register).

Registration Policies

  • Researchers and students are welcome to attend, including those without an accepted conference paper.
  • Each accepted paper must be covered by at least one full registration. A separate full registration is required for each paper, and at least one author of each paper must attend and present it in person. See the camera-ready instructions.
  • Student poster participants should select Student Poster Registration in the CCF system. This category does not replace the full registration required for an accepted conference paper. See the student poster registration instructions.
  • Online attendance: TBA
  • Invoice or receipt procedures: TBA

Refunds and Cancellations

Refund applications are enabled in the CCF system until Nov 3, 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8). Requests are subject to the CCF policy displayed in the registration system:

  • If the event cannot be held for reasons attributable to CCF or force majeure, or force majeure prevents attendance, participants may request a full refund or retain eligibility for the next edition.
  • Duplicate payments are refunded in full for the duplicated amount.
  • For cancellation for personal reasons, the deductions are 10% when requested at least 30 days before the event, 20% at least 7 days before, and 30% at least 4 days before. The policy also offers the option to retain eligibility for the next edition.
  • No refund is available for personal cancellation within the three days before the event, including the event day.

For registration or refund questions, contact ijtcsfaw2026pcs@outlook.com. For hotel reservations, see Accommodation; hotel booking and cancellation terms are separate.
