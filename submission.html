Submission Guidelines

Authors should submit a full, anonymous paper presenting original research related to an IJTCS-FAW 2026 track. The paper should state its contributions clearly and provide the technical content, motivation, and comparison with related work needed for a rigorous review. Detailed topics and policies are available in the Call for Papers.

WINE and ISAAC Fast Tracks

Papers submitted to but not accepted at WINE 2026 or ISAAC 2026 may be submitted through the corresponding IJTCS-FAW 2026 Fast Track by Sep 15, 2026 (AoE). Fast-track papers must be within the scope of an IJTCS-FAW 2026 track and satisfy the conference's anonymity, originality, and no-simultaneous-submission requirements.

Use the same IJTCS-FAW 2026 OpenReview submission system as regular submissions and select Fast Track in the submission form.

  • Submit a revised anonymous manuscript using the IJTCS-FAW 2026, WINE 2026, or ISAAC 2026 submission format. The manuscript must comply with the page limit and other requirements of the selected format.
  • Upload a PDF containing the complete, unedited reviews from WINE 2026 or ISAAC 2026, as applicable, as supplementary material.
  • Include a brief Changes and Response statement describing how the revision addresses those reviews. It may be included in the supplementary review PDF.

The supplementary review PDF and response do not count toward the page limit of the selected submission format. The Program Committee may use the prior reviews and may request additional assessment. See the Call for Papers for the full fast-track policy.

Author Checklist

  • The submission presents original and unpublished work.
  • The submission is not under simultaneous review elsewhere.
  • The manuscript does not reveal author names, affiliations, email addresses, or other identifying information.
  • The main paper is no more than 12 single-spaced pages, excluding the title page and references.
  • The paper uses at least 11-point type and one-inch margins, or the standard LNCS layout.
  • Any appendix is clearly marked, and the main paper contains the material needed to evaluate its central claims.
  • Prior work by the authors is cited in a neutral, non-identifying manner.
